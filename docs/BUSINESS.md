# Business model: free core, optional paid service to customers

## Source license and forever-free self-hosting

AIWallet Release Lab v0.9.0 is Apache-2.0. Anyone may run, modify, self-host and redistribute it for commercial or personal use **without an AIWallet subscription, paid seat, cloud activation or license key**. Local FFmpeg QC, Render Watch, human review, portable reports and CLI stay free.

## Optional customer subscription (not yet available to purchase)

AIWallet may charge paying customers **for a separately operated managed cloud service**: hosting, shared workspaces, project reports, managed policies and support. These are proposals and not functionality in the currently shipped local core:

| Indicative plan | Customer price per month | Planned benefit |
|---|---:|---|
| Creator Cloud | Rp149.000 | Managed dashboard, QC report history, shareable reports |
| Studio Workspace | Rp499.000 | Managed multi-project workspace, policy collaboration, human approval history |

Customers can currently register interest on the website with explicit email consent. There is **no checkout, payment tokenization, activation, paid entitlement, subscription charge, auto-renewal, or hosted media-processing backend** in v0.9. Before enabling it, implement tenant isolation, quotas, authorized uploads, security testing, invoicing/cancellation/refunds and approved merchant recurring capabilities. Never imply plans are already available or take payment without verified customer authorization.

The project owner **does not need to buy a paid SaaS subscription or hosting tier** to distribute this code and collect nonbinding interest. Any future operational spend requires separate explicit approval; free tiers have quotas and cannot be represented as unlimited.

## Optional one-time service

A customer may also request paid installation, CI integration or custom policy design with a separate written scope and one-time fee; this never restricts the free software.

## GitHub growth

Aim for genuinely useful documentation, reproductions, clear issue triage, stable CI and organic recommendations. #1 ranking is only an aspiration until defined by category/date and verified. No fake stars or mass outreach.
